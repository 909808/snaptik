    Questions

    Where does the file go after I tap download?

    Into your browser's usual downloads location. On an iPhone or iPad open the Files app and look in Downloads; to add a video to Photos, open it and tap Share → Save Video.

    Why did my link fail?

    The post may be private, deleted, age-restricted or limited to certain countries. TikTok also blocks automated requests from time to time, so if the link opens in your browser, wait a minute and try again.

    Does the video really have no watermark?

    The main download is the clean version TikTok serves to its own player. If TikTok only offers a watermarked file for a post, the button is labelled Watermarked.

    Can I download photo slideshows?

    Yes: all the photos come as one ZIP, and the sound and cover are separate downloads. If a particular post doesn't offer its photos, you'll still get the sound and cover.

    Can I save everything on someone's profile?

    Paste the profile link to see their recent posts, tick the ones you want and download them as a ZIP, up to a few dozen at a time. Posts are fetched one by one with a short pause so TikTok doesn't block the server, so it takes a while. For a complete archive, use the command-line tool described in the project README: it resumes where it left off.

    Can I save captions and comments?

    Captions, yes: every post has a "Caption & details" file, and ZIPs include a spreadsheet and JSON listing each post's caption, date, hashtags, stats and sound. Comments, no: TikTok doesn't offer them in a form this tool can read reliably. Your own comments are in TikTok's "Download your data" in Settings.

    What am I allowed to download?

    Your own videos, or content you have permission to save. Downloads are for personal, offline viewing: don't re-upload other people's work as your own, and respect copyright and TikTok's Terms of Service.

    Do you keep my videos?

    No. Files pass through the server and are gone when the transfer ends (MP3 conversion uses a short-lived temporary file). Recent lookups are remembered in memory for a few minutes to keep things fast.
